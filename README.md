# Meta Ads Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to evaluate advertising performance across key metrics, including impressions, clicks, engagement, conversions, ad spend, and return on investment.
The dashboard provides a complete marketing-funnel view—from awareness and engagement to conversion and purchase—along with demographic, geographic, campaign, and time-based analysis.

## Business Objective

The business requires a performance-tracking report for advertising campaigns running across Facebook and Instagram. The report provides visibility into campaign reach, engagement, conversions, and budget utilization, enabling the marketing team to evaluate campaign effectiveness and make data-driven budget decisions.

## Business Questions

* Which platform—Facebook or Instagram—delivers the strongest campaign performance?

* Which campaigns generate the highest engagement and conversion rates?

* How efficiently is the advertising budget being utilized?

* Which campaigns deliver the highest return on ad spend (ROAS)?

* How do audience engagement and conversion patterns vary across demographic groups, locations, and time periods?
